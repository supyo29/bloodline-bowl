## 2. Existing waiver audit (`lib/weekly/waivers.ts`, 578 lines, `waiver-engine-2026.1`)
**What v1 optimizes.** For every prefiltered free agent it evaluates the FULL (add, drop) pair on a *counterfactual optimal legal lineup* (the shared Hungarian optimizer), picks the best legal drop by `(lineup gain − keep value)`, and ranks by `net_roster_gain = Σ weight·component` (`decision-score.ts`): `starter_upgrade·1.0 + weekly_vor·0.6 + bench_utility·0.25 + positional_scarcity·0.35 + bye_coverage·0.8 + injury_hedge·0.7 + rest_of_season_value·0.45 − drop_cost·1.0 − uncertainty_penalty·1.0`. Priority: `net < 0.75` → DO_NOT_ADD; `net ≥ 4` with a starter/bye/hedge reason → HIGH; `net ≥ 2` → MEDIUM; else LOW. It concludes DO_NOT_ADD, refuses a drop that leaves a slot unfillable, reports `starter_impact_status UNRESOLVED` (never a fake 0) on a projection gap, and is gated on `free_agent_pool_readiness.actionable`.
**Useful certified behavior preserved/consumed (not rebuilt):** the (add, drop) framing; optimal-lineup counterfactuals and their legality rule; UNRESOLVED-not-zero; the readiness gate; `WeeklyTeamContext`; the `weeklyVOR` replacement frontier; deterministic `ctx.generated_at`; canonical scoring via `scoreWeeklyLine`.
**Weaknesses (documented, v1 left untouched):** (1) generic player value — Role, OPP and FI are **not consumed** (`football_intelligence_used_for_numeric_ranking` is a literal `false`); (2) hard-coded unvalidated weights (table above, `clamp(-4,4)`, `keep = vor + 1.2·rosEdge + …`, `MIN_NET 0.75`); (3) drop cost is one scalar — no option value, handcuff/contingency, bye cover, positional depth or re-add probability; (4) no horizons beyond "this week" and one prorated ROS number; (5) no economics — *"FAAB bid sizing is a later phase"*: no budgets, competition, priority cost or visible winning bids; (6) a flat ranked top-8, no alternatives/tiers; (7) one league-pool replacement level; (8) uncertainty is a single `−1.5` flag; (9) the counterfactual lineup helper is a closure inside v1 (Waiver 2.0 calls the shared optimizer instead of copying v1's rules); (10) no historical validation or prospective capture.
**Surfaces:** `GET /api/waivers/{league}/{manager}/week/{week}`, `/api/intelligence`, the orchestrator (`candidates.ts`, `gates.ts`), Book-Ready `waiver.status` (thin, UNAVAILABLE per readiness), Analysis Book waiver chapters. The trade engine is separate and untouched.
**Answer:** v1 optimizes *the net immediate-to-near-term lineup and depth value of an add/drop pair on this roster*, from external weekly/ROS projections and hand-set weights. It is roster-aware but role-blind, market-blind, horizon-blind and price-blind.

## 3. The decision definition
The unit is the **transaction**: `WaiverAction = { manager, candidate, drop (or open spot), acquisition cost {FAAB range | priority}, gross_add_value, drop_cost, acquisition_cost, risk_penalty, net_action_value (+ interval), horizons {next_week, next_3, ros, playoffs, stash}, value_by_kind {starter, bench_option, contingency_payoff, contingency_counted}, exact next-week lineup delta, candidate & drop assets (role, conditional OPP, schedule, uncertainty, archetype), market {scarcity, competitors, faab, priority}, alternatives, tier A/B/C/PASS, confidence, components, content_id }`. **PASS ("make no claim") is a first-class action.** Nothing is submitted.

## 7. Roster-value model
One function values ANY player against MY roster in league-scoring points per week, summed over horizons with a per-week discount. **Level** = current projection blended toward ROS/week over 4 weeks; + persisted Role change; + established OPP (next week only); × availability; bye = 0. **Margin** = what he must beat: for a candidate or my bench player, the marginal starter he could displace (from the optimal lineup: slot-eligibility aware, FLEX/SUPERFLEX correct); for my starter, his best bench cover. `starter_value = Σ max(0, level_w − margin)·avail·discount` over the 6-week decision horizon. The exact **next-week optimal-lineup counterfactual** replaces the asset model's next-week estimate whenever it differs by >1 point (lineup stays the source of truth; UNRESOLVED on a projection gap, never 0).

## 8. Replacement value
Per position: obtainable free-agent replacement (consumed nth-best-available framework), my starter baseline, my rostered replacement, bench replacement, and **scarcity = 1/(1+free agents at or above my starter baseline)**. A candidate is valued above what this roster can actually obtain, not above zero. A replacement level derived from a *fallback* (no free agent exists at that position) is **not treated as obtainable** (`fa_basis`) — this fixed a real defect found during Checkpoint H (depth was being priced against an unobtainable level).

## 9. Drop cost
`drop_cost = starter value lost (vs best remaining cover) + bench option lost + established contingency lost + bye-cover lost − re-add credit`, every part exposed (`drop.*` components). Positional **depth** is priced as `weeks × P(needed | position) × (level − max(cover, obtainable replacement))` with position priors (QB .07 … RB .16). Example (test): with the natural drop `r4` (a spare RB), adding a free-agent RB nets **7.99**; make `r4` valuable and the engine drops `r3` instead and the net falls to **0.61** — the drop changes the action. A roster whose bench is all non-redundant, similarly valuable assets turns a positive-looking add negative (net < 1.5, no recommendation).

## 10. Optionality
Three separate kinds: **starter value**, **bench option** (role growth × weight, variance upside × P(start), depth-chart uncertainty, positional depth) and **contingency** (counted only when established, else reported as `contingency_payoff` with zero weight). Emerging or rookie players show bench-option value; they are not forced into a weekly-start frame.

## 11. Schedule / time horizon
Five horizons reported separately: `next_week`, `next_3`, `ros`, `playoffs`, `stash`. Byes come from the NFL schedule; games remaining and known playoff opponents are exposed. A veteran can win `next_week` while a rising player wins `playoffs` (tested); a two-week injury fill gains only its established next-week delta (`next_3` gains the same single week — the window is not extrapolated). Deep player-specific matchup value is **Phase 5**.

## 12. Opportunity Propagation integration (conditional)
OPP is consumed via `evaluateOpportunityPropagationScenario` and stays CONDITIONAL. It counts toward value **only when an official availability designation of a teammate establishes the condition**, weighted by the designation's chance of missing (OUT/IR 1.0, DOUBTFUL 0.8, QUESTIONABLE 0.25), for the next week only, and raises the `injury_contingency` uncertainty. Otherwise the payoff is reported as `opp.conditional_payoff` with **zero weight** (tested: gross value is identical with and without the unestablished scenario). OPP is never used to estimate whether anyone will be unavailable.

## 13. Football Intelligence integration
FI opponent readings (`def_pass/rush_epa_allowed`, percentile, predictive status) are **explanatory only**: `fi_numeric: false`, `fi_adjustment_points = 0` (a test pins this). Numeric use awaits validation (Phase 5 matchup work). FI's PARTIAL week and Role lag feed the `source_lag` uncertainty.

## 14. Scoring integration
Opportunity→points uses the **canonical scoring engine** (`scoreWeeklyLine` on a typical per-target and per-carry stat line with the league's `raw_scoring`): PPR vs standard changes a target's worth (tested: PPR target > standard target; carries unchanged; different `scoring_fingerprint`). Role growth is worth more in a PPR league. **Unsupported propagation (documented for Phase 6):** threshold/bonus categories that need a whole game line (e.g. 100-yard bonuses), position-specific scoring beyond rec/rush, and return-yard scoring for Role return shares are not converted by the per-opportunity path; weekly projections are already in league scoring.

## 15. Market competition
For each other team: marginal starter at the position (slot-aware), the candidate's gap over it, unfilled slots, starters with injury designations or a bye within 2 weeks, budget. Output `CompetitorView{need_strength, marginal_starter_gap, positional_weakness, budget, evidence}`. **Structural only: intended bids are never inferred**; competitors' unseen budgets are labelled, not guessed.

## 16. FAAB
A range — `min_useful / expected_competitive / aggressive / walk_away` (dollars) — not rank→percentage. Inputs: my surplus (net before acquisition cost), **my budget's shadow price** (points per dollar = my top-3 distinct alternatives' surplus ÷ remaining budget, so alternatives raise the price of any one claim), structural demand, candidate desirability (rank among my targets), and **visible winning bids** when present (`VISIBLE_WINNING_BIDS`) else an explicit `UNCALIBRATED_PRIOR`. Bounds: walk-away ≤ 0.9 × value bought and ≤ 60% of remaining; never above my budget. Example (test league): FA WR → `[$6, $14, $37, $60]`; add two comparable alternatives and the walk-away falls to **$36**; with visible winning bids the expected bid is far above the prior. The **acquisition cost in points** = expected competitive bid × shadow price. Rolling/reverse-standings leagues get `priority.advice ∈ {SPEND, HOLD}` and a priority cost instead.

## 17. Action score
`net_action_value = gross_add_value − drop_cost − acquisition_cost − risk_penalty (+ exact lineup adjustment)`; `gross_add_value = starter + bench_option + established contingency` over the 6-week decision horizon; `risk_penalty = 0.6 × uncertainty × gross`. Every term is a named component; tests assert the components sum to the net. Tiers: **A** net ≥ 3 and lower bound > 0; **B** ≥ 1.25; **C** ≥ 0.75 (speculative); **PASS** below. Ranking is deterministic (net, then lower acquisition cost, then lower uncertainty, then id). A cheaper near-equivalent can outrank a costlier marginal upgrade on net (tested: a 10.9-pt WR beats an 11.0-pt WR because he is cheaper to acquire, and is surfaced as the alternative).

## 18. Uncertainty
Eight named components with reasons and weights (`role`, `sample`, `projection`, `source_lag`, `pool` certification, `injury_contingency`, `ros_disagreement`, `depth_chart`) → `total ∈ [0,1]` and a value interval (`net_low/net_high`). Not a calibrated interval and labelled as such. An uncertified pool sets `pool = 1`; Role through week 0 vs week 5 raises `source_lag`.

## 28. Limitations

2. **No live actionable output**: the free-agent pool is not certified in production, so nothing can be recommended; live validation used an uncertified pool.
3. Opportunity volumes and per-target/carry yields are **league-wide priors**; FI has no numeric weight; realized fantasy points are not wired (`recent_points` is optional input — fluke detection is UNAVAILABLE without it).
4. FAAB bids are anchored on an **uncalibrated prior** unless winning bids are visible; other managers' budgets can be missing (labelled).
5. Second-order roster effects (a displaced starter becoming cover) are approximated by the exact next-week lineup delta only for week 1.
6. Threshold scoring bonuses and return scoring are not converted (Phase 6).
7. Analysis Book waiver chapters are PARTIAL (pool-gated); request-scoped topics each rebuild the weekly context (~0.5–1.4 s each).
8. Capture persistence is prepared but not applied or wired; no Book-Ready retrieval of captures.
9. Book-Ready responses repeat lineage per block (245 KB for the actions topic).

## M10. Wiring, isolation of the write path, and the write-bound finding
The write is **telemetry after the evaluation is finished**, through a one-way hook (`lib/waiver2/capture-hook.ts`) installed by `app/api/evidence/route.ts`; Book-Ready never imports persistence. It cannot alter the response: every error is swallowed by the hook, counted in `capture_health`, and a store failure/timeout is never counted as evidence collected (tests: throwing, erroring and hanging stores; evaluation and Book-Ready output byte-identical). Illustrative requests write nothing. Uncertified pool ⇒ only a `NOT_ACTIONABLE` readiness row. Certified pool ⇒ a live lock check decides LIVE_CAPTURED / LIVE_POST_LOCK / LIVE_UNVERIFIED — **no code change is needed when the pool becomes certified** (tested with a certified fixture).
**Finding fixed during verification:** the first deploy wrote a new blocked row per request because the canonical snapshot id changes on every read — unbounded write amplification from a public route. Fixed in `b510813`: a blocked record's identity excludes the per-read snapshot id, and the runtime throttles one attempt per (league, manager, week, class) per 10 minutes per instance. **Live proof:** 36 further strict requests added exactly **2** rows (one new-identity readiness row per manager). The 4 rows written by the first deploy are immutable and remain (valid but redundant); final table state: **6 rows — all `NOT_ACTIONABLE`/`POOL_READINESS_BLOCKED`, 0 ranked-action rows, 0 live, 0 illustrative**, each with scoring fingerprint, snapshot id, Role/FI versions, schedule identity and the pool reason code.

## M11. Outcome attachment and the evidence gate
Outcomes attach separately (`WaiverOutcome`: claim result, winning bid, winning manager, candidate unclaimed, realized starts/drop/role/survival/alternative points), insert-only, FK-bound, and never mutate a capture or feed back into confidence. **Eligibility** (`evaluationEligibility`): LIVE_CAPTURED, ranked-actions, certified + actionable pool, `scoring:v1:` fingerprint, complete snapshot + Role/FI/schedule identity, non-empty actions, verified pre-kickoff lock, roster identity that re-verifies, valid record, **and** an outcome. Thresholds fixed before any outcome exists — **minimum**: 8 distinct live weeks, 60 eligible decisions, 3 managers; **preferred**: 14 weeks, 200 decisions, 6 managers, 2 leagues. Meeting a threshold only makes evidence **reviewable** (`MINIMUM_/PREFERRED_MET_FOR_REVIEW`); `auto_promotion: false`, and the gate cannot change the lifecycle state. **Eligible count today: 0** (`NOT_ELIGIBLE`; 6 excluded readiness rows).
