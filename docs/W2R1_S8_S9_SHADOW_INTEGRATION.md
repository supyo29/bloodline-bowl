## 4. Prospective Dynamic-VOR validation plan (real rosters, from Week 6/7)
Historical synthetic rosters cannot certify private roster value. For every captured (league, manager, week): capture the real legal roster, bench, IR/PUP, verified waiver pool, league scoring fingerprint, lineup constraints and acquisition state **before the first kickoff**; freeze with the manifest. After the week, grade with realized points only: predicted Weekly Roster VOR vs realized marginal lineup value (best realized legal lineup with the asset minus without it plus the recommended replacement); realized performance of the recommended replacement; **decision regret** = best realized verified-pool alternative minus the realized recommended replacement; **bye preservation** (a bye asset's Hold VOR must stay ≥ 0); whether **high private-VOR assets prove hard to replace** (realized marginal ≥ threshold) and whether **low/negative-VOR assets are genuinely replaceable**. No roster is optimized or edited after outcomes; the frozen belief is the only thing graded. Minimum before any claim: ≥ 4 weeks × ≥ 5 managers; report error, regret and both rates with cluster-bootstrap intervals; nothing is promoted.

## 5. S9 performance
Profiling (synthetic league: 19 rostered, 160 pool candidates, 12-week horizon; pure compute, `scripts/w2r1/perf`): frozen Dynamic VOR **≈ 750 ms** for a full roster pass; the dominant cost is the optimizer being re-run on identical scenarios (≈ 18 k scenario calls, 40 % duplicate). A per-request, identity-scoped memo (`scenario-cache.ts`, generated `vor-fast.ts` derived from the frozen `vor.ts`, outputs deep-equal to the frozen implementation on 8 random leagues × every roster player) cuts it to **≈ 470 ms (−38 %)**; the remaining cost is distinct counterfactual scenarios (~10 k × 0.1 ms). Freshness is untouched (nothing survives a request). **Yahoo latency cannot be profiled without credentials**; the route now returns `context_ms / adapter_ms / compute_ms` and Dynamic-VOR compute was ~1–1.6 s of the 4–9 s warm / 10–17 s cold Yahoo path (a ~38 % compute saving is therefore ≈ 0.4–0.6 s), so the 1.5 s warm target is **not reachable by compute work alone** — it depends on Yahoo request fan-out and cross-request league/market reuse, to be profiled on the durable staging environment with the new timing fields. Safe next steps (not done, need live profiling): batch Yahoo calls, share the market-state load across managers of one league within a short TTL keyed by provider revision, and reuse identity resolution per league.
